support-audit: can a support set validate itself?

Ledoit-WolfLOOBreakdown

A small, CPU-only audit for few-shot evaluation. It computes the shrunk-precision leverage ηi, the mean breakdown parameter ν, ranks four heads by exact leave-one-out accuracy, and learns a regime-specific safe/unsafe boundary from labelled calibration audits.

  • Fixed CSV contract: audit_id, nu, max_eta, loo_best_model, safe.
  • Reference evidence on 64 synthetic audits: 0.9375 overall safe accuracy, 0.75 worst-regime, 64/64 exact LOO winners.
  • Query labels are never read at audit time.

Under review · TMLR 14922

When Can a Support Set Validate Itself? A Computable Breakdown Parameter

Statistical theoryValidationModel selection

The central claim is a mechanism, not a new SOTA: deleting one support point is a rank-one downdate of the shrunk precision, so the fold gap splits into a mean pull and a precision-feedback term. The paper separates error in level from error in ranking, and gives a closed-form correction that matches exhaustive refit without refitting.

  • Rank-one identity closes to 3.1e-12 on 6,048 held-out points.
  • Closed-form correction: catastrophic 8-shot selections fall from 41 to 4 out of 400.
  • Boundary claim is explicit: a computable diagnostic, not a distribution-free guarantee.

Selective naming under calibration contamination

EEGSelective riskContaminationCertification

2026 — CASCADE (BSPC, submitted 25 Nov 2026). A certificate + margin + NACT pipeline for EEG lead localization. At 5.2% contamination, the max-score baseline drops to 0.0008 correct naming while NACT retains 0.2346; at 24.1%, NACT retains 0.1773. The result is a controlled-fault audit on public EEGMMIDB, not a clinical validation.

2027 — AG-SSN / CAAG (BSPC, submitted 25 Nov 2026). A structure-projected selective state-space model with a label-free contamination estimator and an asymmetric gate. The supported claim is decision-layer selective safety, not clean-accuracy superiority: 0.592 vs 0.654 filterbank, FASS p = 0.515.

Both manuscripts keep the same boundary: synthetic episodes plus a ten-subject real pilot with injected faults; no technician-annotated clinical cohort.
